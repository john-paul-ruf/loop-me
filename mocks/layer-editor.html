layer-editor.html — FR-6, FR-10, FR-11. The central design problem: an animatable parameter is four values (min, max, cycles, curve), and there are hundreds of them. Solution — a dual-thumb band on the parameter's hard bounds plus a live cyan tick showing the value the engine is resolving right now. The tick moves as you watch, so the abstract four-value model becomes something you can see. Cycles and curve hide behind per-parameter Advanced, collapsed by default. Above all of it, six Motion characters set every curve on the layer at once — that's the simple surface. Bounds are printed under every track, so a control can never be driven somewhere ugly.
